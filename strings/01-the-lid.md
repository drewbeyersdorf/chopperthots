## What the world had

- 1960 Licklider — man-computer symbiosis. a second nervous system. not a servant.
- 1962 / 68 Engelbart — augment the human. the demo was a lid, not a replacement head.
- 1966 Weizenbaum — ELIZA nodded and people fell in love. he begged us not to marry the doll.
- 1983 Bainbridge — ironies of automation. you automate the easy. the human rusts on the weird leftovers.
- 2019 Sutton — the bitter lesson. general methods plus compute beat your clever story. it hurts because it’s true.

They had this. Most of them forgot before the poster. We recap it anyway.
