# String 07 — biomimicry and the goo

## Biomimicry

one nest. one egg. care is prune. feed is how you get thirty-five children and none of them hunt.
the saltie does not run a platform. she holds a bank.

## Ontological hierarchy

types live upstairs. goo lives in the bottom drawer.
do not promote the goo. do not mash two drawers so the night feels finished.
the hierarchy is not a vibe. it is which shelf may hold the thing.

## What the world had

- Uexküll — the animal has a world, not a spreadsheet.
- von Neumann — the constructor copies a type, not a puddle.
- Palantir-style ontology, said plainly: objects, links, properties. goo is what you have before you earned those.
- Grains: two ledgers stay two. that is hierarchy with mud on it.
