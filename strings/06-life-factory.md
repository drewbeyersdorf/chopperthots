# String 06 — from the 40s to Life to the factory

Two streams. They rhyme. They are not father and son. Recap both before someone sells you a new factory.

## Stream A — automation that learned to copy itself

- 1943 McCulloch & Pitts — a nerve as a logic gate. the first thin machine in a skull.
- 1945–48 the automatic computers leave the war. ENIAC. ACE. the tape starts moving for real.
- 1948 Wiener — *Cybernetics*. feedback. animal and machine on the same page. control is a loop, not a speech.
- 1948 von Neumann — “The General and Logical Theory of Automata.” a machine that can make a machine.
- ~1951 Ulam tells him to stop building a 3d factory in equations and use a grid of cells.
- 1952–53 von Neumann’s cellular automaton. 29 states. a universal constructor. self-reproduction as a proof, not a vibe.
- 1966 Burks finishes the book after von Neumann is gone.
- 1968–70 Conway hunts a smaller rule. *Life* lands in Gardner’s *Scientific American*, October 1970.
- simple neighbors. live, die, stay. gliders. guns. a Turing machine hiding in the foam.

local rule. global weather. that is the whole trick. we still sell it as a miracle every spring.

## Stream B — the shop that wanted software to be a line

- same decades: factories already knew feedback. Wiener walked out of the war with it.
- 1969 Hitachi names a software organization a factory. first to put the word on the door.
- 70s–80s Japan leans on process. reuse. the line, not the lone genius. Fujitsu and the rest follow.
- 1991 Cusumano writes it down so the West can stop pretending it invented process.
- then CASE tools. then agile said the line was a lie. then the agent room put the word *factory* back on the poster.

## Where they actually touch

Life is not Hitachi. Hitachi is not a glider gun.

What they share: **a small rule, repeated, until a shop appears.**
Von Neumann wanted a constructor that copies the constructor.
A software factory wants a process that copies the process.
An agent factory wants a spec that copies the next spec.

If your factory needs a priest in every cell, it is not Life. it is a hotel.

## Our cuts

1. the 40s already had the loop. we just put a nicer jacket on the ghost.
2. don’t try to invent the factory. do the hours. the grid was drawn.
3. buy the ticket from Wiener. stage the ride through Conway. don’t punch Hitachi drunk.
4. neighbors update. the highway is just a bigger grid. fog is the uncomputed cell.
5. the lineup is cellular. you only see the three next to you. the set still peaks.
6. years on this. still no auto-commit. a constructor that cannot stop is not a shop. it’s a cancer.

## Use when

Someone says agent factory like it was born last Tuesday.
Someone says emergence like Conway didn’t already waste a decade of coffee on it.
Someone wants the form to wait on a self-copying node.
