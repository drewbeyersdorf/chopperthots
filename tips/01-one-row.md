# One row. Type stays blank until you say.

i sit with one row like a bad check. i do not name the animal until the man at the end of the bar nods. the sheet stays blank. that’s the only honesty left in the room.

## When

You have one ticket. The type is still empty. The form just arrived.

## What

A ritual, not a classifier. The agent may draft. It may not stamp the type.

## How

You start it. Submit does not start it. It may call “if nobody said it.” It may not send the work onward.

## Use when

One open ticket. Someone asks “what type is this.” You refuse to auto-label on intake.

Never fire on form submit. Never write the type until a human says.
