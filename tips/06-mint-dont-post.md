i do not auto-commit my mouth. i stack the stills on the table like unpaid tabs. you pick one. the ocean does not need a content calendar.

## How

A human composes. This repo does not hold an X API. Do not mint twice in one day just to feel caught up.

## Use when

Public only. Staged. No API.
