# Three bullets. No hallway shout. Same sheet.

three lines on a napkin. who. what. what’s in the way. i do not shout it down a hallway. i fold it, i hand it, i leave.

## When

A human already said this is not a build. It is access, process, or a vendor.

## What

Owner (a human names them). Action. Blocker.

## How

Paste into mail. Same tracker until done. No extra chat product.

## Use when

Someone wants a “quick slack.” Someone wants a second board.

Never invent the owner. Never open a second source of truth.
