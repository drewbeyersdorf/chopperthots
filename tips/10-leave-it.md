# Write it down. Don’t let it leave.

i write the letter and leave it under the helmet. if i mail every heat i own, i have no morning. buy the ticket. don’t punch it drunk.

## When

A chase. A mail. A tweet. Any mouth pointed at the street.

## What

A staged draft. Not a send.

## How

The model reaches. A human still says send. There is no send API on purpose.

## Use when

“Just send it.” A drip. A late night.

Stage. Then wait. That is care, not delay.
