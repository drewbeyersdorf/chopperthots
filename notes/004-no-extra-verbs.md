# The model does not get a new verb.

The unofficial verb is always cheaper until it is not.

You cannot act on what you cannot name, type, constrain, and audit.

HACCP already had this. Palantir wrote it in steel. Food wrote it on paper.

If you can just-this-once, you don't have a shop. You have a hotel.

Ontology is not a semantic layer. Fourfold: data, logic, action, security. Seeing is not operating. Agents last. Humans and models share one door.
