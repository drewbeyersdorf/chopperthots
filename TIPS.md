# A tip is the atom

Every AI thing becomes a tip. Headline first. Then only the lenses that are actually strong for *this* one.

History · biomimicry · ontology · business · advice · lived

If a lens is weak, leave it out. Don't stuff six. The mix is the reasoning, not a form.

Same skill can have many notes. Different angle, different day. That is not repetition-as-failure. That is the magazine.

```
tips/03-spec-stays.md          ← the tip
  notes/003a-spec-is-a-contract.md
  notes/003b-hitachi-already.md
  notes/003c-blank-cell.md
```

X: the headline, or one angle.
Short: the strongest image in the mix.
LinkedIn: the business angle, only if it earned it.
Blog: another article on the same skill.

Lived still fail-closed. No invented hours.
