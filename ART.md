# Art lock

Smart customers can see a fake. We do not ship one.

## Only these banks

1. **Daytona Beach, Florida** — real biker / Bike Week / beach-race photographs.
2. **Old Santa Monica beach** — real archival beach and surf photographs. **Not Santa Cruz. Not Pacifica.**
3. **Coveted computer-science archive** — real plates. ENIAC, automata, labs. Public domain or clearly licensed.

## How

Edit only. Crop, grade, dust. Do not invent a bike that was not in the frame. Do not generate a 1948 lab.

If the archive is rate-limited, wait.

## Credit

No still without author, year, license.
