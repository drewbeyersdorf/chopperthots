Faceless AI explainers and HistoryInPics are the anti-models. Unsourced. Untrue. Obviously generated. Smart customers leave.

## What they share

1. A constraint (one archive, one format, one move).
2. Credit or silence. Never fake provenance.
3. The person is not in the frame.
4. Repetition is the brand. They do not reinvent the show.
5. The desk is a queue, not inspiration.

## Your desk

Three banks. One thought. Four mouths.

```
plate (credited) + line (Drew)
        → note  (blog, home)
        → string (X)
        → short (YouTube)
        → graf  (LinkedIn, if the customer needs it)
```

Weekly issue = the exhibition. Daily pack = the hanging.

You do not need to be Archillect. You need to be Public Domain Review with a motorcycle.

## Daily curate (20 minutes)

1. Pick one plate from a bank or wait. Do not generate a stand-in.
2. Write the line like you’re at the table. If it sounds like a poster, delete it.
3. Hang it on all four mouths from that pair. Repeat is fine.
4. File credit in `public/thots/sources/CREDITS.md` or skip the picture.
5. After 24h: smart reply / rewatch / customer note. Not likes.

## Do not curate

Your face. Santa Cruz. Pacifica. Dead scientists as deepfakes. Hashtags. Five lessons. Medium-first. Auto-post without a cred.
